Disk health

Disk health (My Files › Folders › Disk health) answers the question "what is filling my disk?" and helps you free space safely: everything goes to the Trash first.

Analysing a place#

  1. Under Place to analyse, choose Home or a drive.
  2. Press Analyse. The analysis runs in the background; the window stays usable.

Duplicate files#

Press Find duplicate files. Files of 1 MB or more in the chosen place (Home or a drive) are compared by content (size first, then a SHA-256 hash), never only by name. In each group the oldest copy is kept and the others come already marked. Backups made by FileExp are not counted as duplicates.

Freeing space#

  1. Check the files you no longer need (or keep the automatic marks in duplicates).
  2. Press Move to Trash. Ctrl+Z brings them back.
  3. The Trash keeps a compressed copy, so the space is only freed when you press Empty the Trash.
